Team

Paul Williams

Advisory Board Member

Paul Williams is a successful entrepreneur, executive, and investor with more than two decades of leadership experience across the energy, entertainment, sports, and transportation industries. He currently serves as Chairman of the Board and Chief Executive Officer of Stallion Oil Corporation.

Mr. Williams founded Stallion Energy Group, LLC, where he developed oil and gas assets throughout Texas and California. Prior to Stallion, he served as Chief Executive Officer and Director of Eagle Ford Oil & Gas, a publicly traded company. Earlier in his career, he was the Managing Partner of Vaquero Oil & Gas, where he led the development and drilling of numerous South Texas wells in partnership with a publicly traded energy company with an enterprise value approaching $1 billion. Throughout his career, he has originated, financed, and managed multi-million-dollar oil and gas projects, negotiated strategic joint ventures, and overseen major seismic exploration programs.

Beyond the energy sector, Mr. Williams has built and invested in businesses across a wide range of industries, including professional sports, manufacturing, transportation, and entertainment. His investments have included a professional minor league baseball franchise, a trailer manufacturing company, a moving and storage business, and multiple motion picture projects. He also participated in a mixed martial arts venture that collaborated with Mark Cuban’s HDNet, providing event footage and contributing strategic input on fighter rankings and event programming.

Mr. Williams brings to the Advisory Board extensive experience in executive leadership, strategic planning, capital formation, business development, and corporate governance. His diverse background in building companies, raising capital, negotiating complex transactions, and identifying growth opportunities provides valuable perspective to organizations seeking long-term success and sustainable enterprise value.

In addition to his business career, Mr. Williams was the Republican nominee for the United States Congress in Texas’s 9th Congressional District in both 2000 and 2002. Earlier in his career, he played professional football as a quarterback with the Houston Oilers organization and in the Arena Football League.

Mr. Williams is married to Kelly Williams and has two children.

Jack Sarsen

Advisory Board Member

Jack Sarsen brings a strategic and creative edge to Triple Martini Productions, contributing across business development, ideation, and leadership. With a background in advertising and executive-level marketing, he has led major campaigns at industry giants like Ogilvy & Mather and Grey and spearheaded strategic growth initiatives at Gartner and Wolters Kluwer. John Shattuck

Creative Development Executive

John Shattuck is a former US Navy Airman, serial entrepreneur, humanitarian, hostage negotiator, border security expert, military advocate and creative development executive at Triple Martini Productions.

For more than two decades he has dedicated his life to international humanitarian work, coordinating the shipment of dozens of cargo containers filled with medical supplies, food, clothing, school equipment, and essential aid to some of the world's most underserved communities. Best known for his extensive relief efforts in Haiti following the devastating 2010 earthquake, Shattuck has worked alongside hospitals, nonprofits, governments, and international organizations, helping deliver life-saving resources while also leading successful efforts to secure the release of several imprisoned American aid workers from Haitian prisons, a story that earned international recognition and praise for his courage and determination.

His humanitarian work has also extended to countries including Niger, El Salvador, Honduras, Nicaragua, Guatemala, Dominican Republic, Mexico, Iraq and Afghanistan where he has built partnerships that continue to improve lives. In recent years he was pivotal in extracting hundreds of Americans left behind in Afghanistan during the exodus working with then Fox News anchor Pete Hegseth now Secretary of War. Today, Shattuck brings that same passion for service, leadership, and problem-solving to Triple Martini Productions, where he helps develop films and documentaries that seek to inspire audiences and create meaningful impact around the world.
